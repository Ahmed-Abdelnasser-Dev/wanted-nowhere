# Player

the player moves using the mouse right click and attack melee attack using the mouse left click

player has a 3 attack combo that happens (slash, slash, triple slash at once).

the player has 4 abilities

**Normal Phase:**
Q: Shadow Movement => a dash that damages and knocks back the enemies.
W: Bat Shape/Human Shape => transform to bat where u are faster, smaller, takes less damage form the sun and immune for the first 2 sec, u can transform back to human shape but u loose all of these effects.
E: Blood Blast => a blast that takes the shape of a circle that damages the enemies and applies slow to them for 0.5 sec
R: Ascend => u take a higher form where u are stronger, faster, has some ghosts as minions, have better version of the abilities and can ignore the sun damage, this lasts for 30 sec and u have a 90 sec cool down

**Ascended Phase:**

Q: Death Dance: while dashing spikes comes out of the ground that damages the enemies even more, u can dash 2 times in a row.
W: Wake the Dead: Summon ghosts that fight with u.
E: Blink of the Death: execute enemies in a small radius and slow and damage in a outer radius.
R: ends your ascendtion early, based on the time left of the abilty it gets cut from the cooldown for the ultmate.
