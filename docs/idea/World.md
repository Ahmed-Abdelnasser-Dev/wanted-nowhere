# World

The world would be a big open map. and it will have different buildings, different terrains, different sections. It will have a forest, a city, a castle, some games, and sea or water. The map itself will also have shadows in different places. So during the day, the vampire can use these shadows to avoid the sun damage. and navigate through them using the dash ability.

during a boss fight, the map will change to have some borders such as arena so the player can't move freely in the big fight. It will be restricted by some rocks, wood, or any other thing related to this.

The game will take place in a fantasy world where it has knights, archers and holy magicians or whatever they are called, as well as monsters that will also fight the player such as zombie skeletons, orcs, and enemies of these sorts.
